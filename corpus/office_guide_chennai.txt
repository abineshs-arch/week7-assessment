Yoho Technologies - Chennai Office Guide

Section 1: Building
The Chennai office has 3 floors. The office head is Priya Raman.

Section 2: Canteen
The canteen is open from 7:30 AM to 8:00 PM. Lunch is served from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM.

Section 3: Parking
There are 40 car parking slots. Two-wheeler parking is in the basement.

Section 4: Security and first aid
Call security on extension 4100. The first-aid room is on floor 1.

Section 5: Shuttle
The office shuttle leaves the nearest metro station every 15 minutes from 8 AM to 10 AM.
