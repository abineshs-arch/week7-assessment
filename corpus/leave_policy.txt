Yoho Technologies - Employee Leave Policy (2026)

Section 1: Annual Leave
Every full-time employee gets 18 days of paid annual leave per year. A maximum of 6 unused days can be carried forward to the next year. Annual leave must be applied at least 7 days in advance through the HRMS portal.

Section 2: Sick Leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 continuous days. Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward.

Section 3: Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 8 days per month. WFH requests must be approved by the reporting manager by 6 PM the previous day.

Section 4: Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 12 working days, to be used within 3 months of the child's birth.

Section 5: Leave Encashment
At the time of resignation, a maximum of 15 unused annual leave days can be encashed at the basic salary rate.
Section 6: Bereavement Leave
Employees get 3 days of paid bereavement leave.
Employees must inform HR within 24 hours of a bereavement.
