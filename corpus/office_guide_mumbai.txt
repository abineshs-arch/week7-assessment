Yoho Technologies - Mumbai Office Guide

Section 1: Building
The Mumbai office has 3 floors. The office head is Sneha Shah.

Section 2: Canteen
The canteen is open from 7:30 AM to 9:00 PM. Lunch is served from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM.

Section 3: Parking
There are 100 car parking slots. Two-wheeler parking is in the basement.

Section 4: Security and first aid
Call security on extension 4104. The first-aid room is on floor 2.

Section 5: Shuttle
The office shuttle leaves the nearest metro station every 20 minutes from 8 AM to 10 AM.
