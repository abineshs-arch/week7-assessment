Yoho Technologies - Hyderabad Office Guide

Section 1: Building
The Hyderabad office has 5 floors. The office head is Kavya Reddy.

Section 2: Canteen
The canteen is open from 7:30 AM to 10:00 PM. Lunch is served from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM.

Section 3: Parking
There are 70 car parking slots. Two-wheeler parking is in the basement.

Section 4: Security and first aid
Call security on extension 4102. The first-aid room is on floor 3.

Section 5: Shuttle
The office shuttle leaves the nearest metro station every 25 minutes from 8 AM to 10 AM.
